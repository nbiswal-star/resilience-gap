# Data-quality log

The pipeline's checks are defined in `config/dq_checks.yml` (**75 checks**), and every execution
is recorded in `ops.dq_results`: failing rows, total, ratio, threshold, status, and a sample of
offending keys. The HTML report shows the latest run.

This page is the **issue register**: what we found, the evidence, and the decision taken. Counts
are from the national run (2000–2025, all states and territories) on 2026-11-08 unless marked
*sample* or *historical*.

**Current status: all 75 checks passed on 2026-11-08**, on the fixture (`make offline-demo`) and
on the national 2000–2025 data (no-download rebuild; DQ about 17 s). Issue 43 (the
storm/declaration side of the Connecticut geography break) added 4 checks (71 → 75) and changed 3
(`serving_damage_reconciles`, `high_need_low_obligation_flag_exact`,
`hma_damage_burden_reconciles`). *Historical:* the previous code (the HMA-only Connecticut fix,
71 checks) passed all 71 on the national data and on the fixture; issues 38–42 were found in
that final review, and their 7 new checks and 6 changed checks
(`hma_positive_obligation_matches_source`, `hma_stage_exclusive_exhaustive`,
`hma_period_reconciles_profile`, `high_need_low_obligation_flag_exact`,
`hma_stage_summary_reconciles`, `hma_counties_not_observed`) are among the 75. The intermediate
HMA funnel run before that passed the 64 checks that existed then. See
[`verification.md`](verification.md).

Severity: **critical** = stop the run; **warn** = continue, must stay under its threshold and be
explained; **info** = expected characteristic, documented.
